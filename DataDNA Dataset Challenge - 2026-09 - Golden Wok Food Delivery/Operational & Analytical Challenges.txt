Operational & Analytical Challenges
Urban food-delivery operations face growing pressure to balance delivery speed, customer experience, traffic conditions, and order profitability, and this dataset highlights several critical operational and analytical challenges:

Fragmented visibility across orders, kitchens, delivery zones, traffic conditions, weather, and customer outcomes makes it difficult to understand overall delivery performance and operational efficiency.
Delivery performance varies significantly by time of day, with rush-hour orders to outer-ring zones experiencing severe delays and SLA breaches, making peak-period capacity and routing difficult to manage.
Weather conditions can materially increase traffic friction and delivery times, making it challenging to predict and manage service disruptions during Rain and Heavy Rain periods.
Long-distance deliveries create structural profitability pressure, with orders exceeding 8 km generating negative profitability as delivery costs increase faster than order revenue.
Food quality deteriorates as delivery times increase, with longer deliveries associated with lower food temperatures and significantly poorer customer ratings.
Profitability varies considerably across delivery corridors and zones, making it difficult to identify which kitchen-to-zone combinations consistently create financial losses.
Traffic congestion across major urban routes can compound both delivery delays and delivery costs, creating operational inefficiencies that are difficult to isolate without corridor-level analysis.
High order volumes do not always translate into healthy business performance, as delays, excessive delivery costs, SLA breaches, and poor customer experiences can erode order profitability.
Limited visibility into the relationship between delivery distance, traffic friction, weather, delivery time, food temperature, and customer ratings weakens operational and service-level decision-making.
Cross-dimensional interactions (e.g., kitchen × zone × time period × weather × traffic conditions) are complex and often under-analysed, hiding high-risk delivery corridors and optimisation opportunities.
Seasonal, daily, and rush-hour variations in delivery demand and traffic conditions create uneven operational performance that is difficult to manage without structured temporal analysis.
Difficulty connecting delivery performance to order profitability, customer satisfaction, and operational costs limits strategic decisions around pricing, routing, kitchen allocation, delivery zones, and SLA management.
